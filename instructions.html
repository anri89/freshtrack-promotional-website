How to use FreshTrack

FreshTrack is designed to make food tracking simple. Follow these steps to organise your kitchen, reduce waste, and make better use of the food you already have.

01

Create your household

Start by setting up your personal account or shared household. This allows everyone in the home to see the same fridge, freezer, and pantry inventory.

02

Add your food items

Add items manually, scan a receipt, or upload a photo. FreshTrack records the food name, storage location, quantity, and expiry date.

03

Check expiry reminders

Use the dashboard to see which items need using first. FreshTrack highlights food that is close to expiring so users can plan meals before it goes to waste.

04

Get meal ideas

FreshTrack suggests meal ideas based on the ingredients already available, helping users save money and avoid buying unnecessary duplicates.

05

Track your waste impact

When food is used or removed, FreshTrack helps users understand their habits and see how much waste they are reducing over time.

06

Keep your inventory updated

Update items when food is eaten, shared, frozen, or thrown away. Keeping the inventory accurate helps the app give better reminders and suggestions.

Ready to start reducing food waste?

Use FreshTrack regularly to build better food habits, save money, and make your household more sustainable.
